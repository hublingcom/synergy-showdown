Chapter 2 · Foundation & history

Built in Deventer, part of a global group

Drukwerkdeal is an online printer: businesses design and order custom printed products on its website, and the company produces and delivers them.

2005

Deventer roots

Drukwerkdeal is founded in Deventer as a web-to-print company, selling custom print to businesses online.

2014

Joining Vistaprint / Cimpress

Its Deventer parent group (People & Print Group, later renamed Printdeal) is acquired by Vistaprint N.V., which renames itself Cimpress later that year. Drukwerkdeal becomes part of this global group of mass-customization businesses.

Today

Print for Dutch & Belgian businesses

Business cards, flyers, banners, stationery and promotional products, each one customized and ordered online. Production runs from a site in Deventer.

Chapter 4 · Your turn

Fill the press sheet

Printing one customer's 100 business cards on its own press run would waste paper and setup time. That is why online printers commonly combine orders from different customers on one sheet, so one print run serves many customers at once. Tap orders to place them on the press sheet and fill at least 90% of it before sending it to the press.

Chapter 5 · What's behind it

Three ideas behind the factory

01

Mass customization

Every product is personal, but the process is standardized, so small custom orders can be made at mass-production cost.

02

Batching

Combining many small orders onto shared press sheets spreads setup cost and cuts paper waste, the puzzle you just solved.

03

Automation

File checks, routing and order tracking are handled by software, so people focus on running the machines and on quality.
